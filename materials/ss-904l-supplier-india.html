SS 904L vs SS 316L

SS 316L is widely used for corrosion resistance in many environments. SS 904L is selected for more demanding acid, chloride and process conditions where higher nickel and molybdenum content is required.

FAQ

Is SS 904L better than SS 316L?

SS 904L has higher alloy content and is normally selected for more aggressive corrosion conditions. The right choice depends on the application, media and project specification.
