Brass C360 Overview

Brass C360 is a free cutting brass grade commonly reviewed for rods, bars, hex bars and machined parts where machinability is central to the requirement. Rubinox is a stockist, supplier, importer, exporter and project supplier, and can review enquiries based on alloy, temper, dimensions, form and documentation requirement.

Common Product Forms

  • round rods
  • hex bars
  • square bars
  • flats
  • profiles and machined blanks
  • Drawing-based parts or project forms for RFQ review

Standards and Temper Guidance

  • ASTM B16 rod, bar and shape references and buyer specifications where applicable.
  • Confirm temper, finish and mechanical requirement where specified.
  • Equivalent grades should be approved by the buyer or project engineer.
  • Inspection, marking and packing requirements should be stated at enquiry stage.

Applications

  • Precision turned parts and CNC components
  • Fittings, connectors and valve components
  • Fasteners, inserts and threaded parts
  • Electrical and instrument hardware
  • High-volume machining requirements

Brass C360 FAQs

What is Brass C360 used for?

Brass C360 is commonly reviewed for free cutting brass for rods, bars and machining. Final use depends on grade, temper, dimensions, drawing, finish and buyer specification.

Which product forms can be requested in Brass C360?

Common enquiries include round rods, hex bars, square bars, flats, profiles and machined blanks. Buyers should confirm grade, temper, dimensions, quantity and applicable standard.

Which standards should buyers mention for Brass C360?

Buyers should mention the required ASTM, EN, DIN, IS or project specification. Typical references include ASTM B16 rod, bar and shape references and buyer specifications where applicable, depending on product form.

What details are needed for a Brass C360 quotation?

Share grade, temper, form, dimensions, quantity, finish, standard, drawing if applicable, documentation requirement and delivery location.
