Rubinox Metal and Alloys is a Hastelloy X supplier in India, offering Alloy X sheets, plates, coils, strips, pipes, tubes, round bars, rods, rings, flanges and fabricated components for gas turbines, combustion systems, industrial furnaces and other elevated-temperature requirements.
