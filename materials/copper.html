Copper Buying Information

For an accurate quotation, specify grade or UNS designation, temper, conductivity requirement and applicable ASTM or EN specification. Dimensional details should include sheet or plate thickness, tube OD and wall, bar diameter, coil width, length and quantity. Also mention whether cutting, machining, special packing, inspection or third-party testing is required.

Grade Selection

Typical selection includes C101 for high-purity electrical use, C110 for electrical and general fabrication, and C122 for plumbing, heat exchangers and formed tube applications. Final material selection should be confirmed against design conditions, corrosion exposure, strength, fabrication and end-user specifications.

Forms & Processing

Rubinox handles enquiries for sheets, plates, strips, coils, pipes, tubes, round bars, rods, flats and busbar requirements. Availability and minimum quantities vary according to alloy, temper or condition, dimensions and processing requirements.

Applications

Copper is commonly requested for electrical systems, heat exchangers, plumbing, refrigeration, earthing and industrial fabrication. Share operating conditions when the material is intended for critical pressure, temperature, electrical or corrosive service.

Supply & Documentation

Material can be coordinated for delivery across India and export markets. State the required test certificate, traceability, inspection, marking and packing details before quotation so they can be included in the offer.
