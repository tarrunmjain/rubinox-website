Common Copper Grades

  • Copper C101: oxygen-free high-conductivity electrical copper
  • Copper C110: ETP copper for electrical and general fabrication
  • Copper C122: DHP copper for tubes, plumbing, HVAC and heat exchangers
  • Copper C145: tellurium copper for machining and electrical parts
  • Copper C172: beryllium copper for high-strength springs and contacts

Typical Applications

  • Electrical busbars, conductors, switchgear and earthing systems
  • HVAC, refrigeration, plumbing and tube systems where specified
  • Heat exchangers, condensers and thermal components
  • Machined electrical connectors, terminals and conductive parts
  • Industrial fabrication, maintenance and project procurement

Copper Supply FAQs

Which copper grades does Rubinox handle?

Rubinox handles copper enquiries for grades such as C101, C110, C122, C145 and C172. Buyers should share grade or UNS reference, temper, form, dimensions, quantity, standard and delivery location.

What is the difference between C101, C110 and C122 copper?

C101 is oxygen-free copper for high-purity electrical requirements, C110 is ETP copper for general electrical and fabrication use, and C122 is DHP copper often reviewed for tubes, plumbing, HVAC and heat exchanger service.

Which copper grade is used for busbars and earthing?

C110 is commonly reviewed for busbars, earthing and electrical conductor requirements, while C101 may be specified for oxygen-free or higher-purity electrical applications.

Which copper grade is used for HVAC or plumbing tubes?

C122 DHP copper is often reviewed for HVAC, refrigeration, plumbing and heat exchanger tube enquiries when the project standard specifies it.

What details are needed for a copper quotation?

Share copper grade, temper, product form, size, thickness, tube OD and wall, bar diameter, coil width, quantity, standard, conductivity requirement, documentation requirement and delivery location.

Can Rubinox support copper sheets, plates, tubes, busbars and rods?

Rubinox can review enquiries for copper sheets, plates, coils, strips, pipes, tubes, busbars, flats, circles, rods, bars, wire and mesh based on grade, temper, size and documentation requirement.

Can buyers request MTC or inspection support for copper?

Buyers can mention MTC, PMI, inspection, marking, packing or third-party inspection requirements in the RFQ. Documentation support is reviewed where applicable and as per requirement.

Can copper grades be substituted with equivalent grades?

Equivalent grades should be used only when approved by the buyer, project engineer or end user. Conductivity, oxygen content, temper, mechanical properties and standard requirements should be checked before substitution.
