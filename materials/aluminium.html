Buyer Specification Guide

  • Alloy and temper, such as 6061-T6, 6063-T6, 6082-T6, 5052-H32, 5083-H111 or 7075-T6.
  • Product form, dimensions, tolerance, finish and quantity.
  • Applicable ASTM, EN, DIN, IS or project standard where specified.
  • Cutting, machining, packing, marking or inspection requirements.
  • Delivery city, project site or export destination.

RFQ Checklist

  • Grade and temper
  • Form: sheet, plate, coil, bar, rod, tube, pipe or profile
  • Size, thickness, OD, wall thickness or drawing
  • Quantity and delivery timeline
  • MTC, PMI, inspection or packing requirement where applicable
  • Delivery location or export destination

Aluminium Supply FAQs

Which aluminium grades does Rubinox handle?

Rubinox handles aluminium enquiries for common grades such as 6061, 6063, 6082, 7075, 5083 and 5052. Buyers should confirm alloy, temper, product form, dimensions, quantity and standard before quotation review.

What is the difference between Aluminium 6061, 6063 and 7075?

Aluminium 6061 is commonly reviewed for general engineering and structural fabrication, 6063 for architectural extrusion profiles, and 7075 for high-strength engineering parts. Final selection depends on design load, temper, fabrication route and buyer specification.

Can Rubinox review Aluminium 1100, 2024, 3003, 6082 or 8011 enquiries?

Yes. Rubinox can review broader aluminium grade enquiries such as 1100, 2014, 2024, 3003, 3105, 5086, 5454, 5754, 6082, 7050 and 8011 when buyers share alloy, temper, product form, dimensions, quantity, standard and documentation requirements.

Which aluminium grade is used for marine applications?

Aluminium 5083 is often reviewed for marine and welded fabrication requirements when the project specification calls for it. Buyers should share temper, thickness, welding details, inspection needs and end-use environment.

What details are needed for an aluminium quotation?

Share grade, temper, product form, size, thickness, OD, wall thickness, length, quantity, standard, finish, inspection requirement and delivery location. Drawings help for extrusion, machining or project-specific profiles.

Can Rubinox support aluminium sheets, plates, bars, rods, pipes and tubes?

Rubinox can review enquiries for aluminium sheets, plates, coils, bars, rods, pipes, tubes, flats, angles, channels, circles and sections based on alloy, temper, dimensions, quantity and documentation requirement.

Can buyers request MTC or inspection support for aluminium?

Buyers can mention MTC, PMI, inspection, marking, packing or third-party inspection requirements in the RFQ. Documentation support is reviewed where applicable and as per requirement.

Can Rubinox review aluminium export enquiries?

Rubinox can review export enquiries when buyers share destination, product form, grade, temper, dimensions, quantity, packing, documentation and delivery terms.

Is Aluminium 7075 always better than 6061?

No. Aluminium 7075 is selected for high strength when specified, while 6061 is often more suitable for general engineering and fabrication. The right grade depends on strength, corrosion exposure, fabrication and project requirements.
