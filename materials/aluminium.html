Aluminium Buying Information

For an accurate quotation, specify alloy and temper, such as 6061-T6, 6063-T6, 5052-H32, 5083-H111 or 7075-T6. Dimensional details should include thickness, width, length, OD, wall thickness, diameter and required temper. Also mention whether cutting, machining, special packing, inspection or third-party testing is required.

Grade Selection

Typical selection includes 6061 for general engineering, 6063 for extrusions and architectural sections, 5052 for forming, 5083 for marine service, and 7075 where high strength is required. Final material selection should be confirmed against design conditions, corrosion exposure, strength, fabrication and end-user specifications.

Forms & Processing

Rubinox handles enquiries for cut-to-size sheets and plates, bars, rods, pipes, tubes, flats, angles and structural profiles. Availability and minimum quantities vary according to alloy, temper or condition, dimensions and processing requirements.

Applications

Aluminium is commonly requested for fabrication, transport, marine, electrical, architectural and aerospace-related requirements. Share operating conditions when the material is intended for critical pressure, temperature, electrical or corrosive service.

Supply & Documentation
