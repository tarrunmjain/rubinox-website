Stainless Steel Products Available in Mumbai

Buyers can enquire for pipes, tubes, sheets, plates, coils, flanges, fittings, bars, rods, flats, angles and fasteners. Availability depends on grade, size, finish and quantity.

Grades for Fabrication & Industry

Common requirements include SS 304 and 304L for general fabrication, SS 316 and 316L for improved chloride resistance, heat-resistant 309/310 grades, 904L for aggressive chemical service, and Duplex 2205 or Super Duplex 2507 for demanding chloride environments.

Mumbai Dispatch & PAN India Delivery

Our Kalbadevi location supports enquiries from Mumbai fabricators, engineering companies, maintenance teams and project buyers. Material can also be coordinated for dispatch across Maharashtra and other Indian states. Share the delivery postcode, required date and unloading restrictions when requesting delivery coordination in the RFQ.

Quality, Cutting & Documentation

Specify ASTM, ASME, EN or customer standards, required finish, dimensional tolerances and certification. Material Test Certificates, heat-number traceability, cutting requirements, inspection and project-specific packing should be confirmed before order placement.

Who We Supply in Mumbai

Rubinox handles enquiries from fabricators, machine shops, piping contractors, equipment manufacturers, maintenance teams, traders and EPC buyers. Requirements may range from replacement quantities and cut pieces to complete project lists. For faster service, send the item description in a structured format and include the required delivery area within Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Thane or the wider Maharashtra industrial network.
