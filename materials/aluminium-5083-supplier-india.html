Aluminium 5083 Overview

Aluminium 5083 is a marine-oriented aluminium-magnesium alloy often reviewed for seawater-adjacent fabrication, transport, tankage and welded structures where the project standard specifies 5083. Rubinox is a stockist, supplier, importer, exporter and project supplier, and can review enquiries based on alloy, temper, form, dimensions and documentation requirement.

Common Product Forms

  • Sheets, plates, coils and cut pieces where applicable
  • Bars, rods, flats and machined blanks
  • Pipes, tubes, angles, channels and profiles where specified
  • Drawing-based parts or project forms for RFQ review

Standards and Temper Guidance

  • ASTM B209 and marine or project specifications where applicable.
  • Common temper references: H111, H116, H321 and project-specified tempers.
  • Confirm any ASTM, EN, DIN, IS or project-specific standard in the RFQ.
  • Equivalent grades or alternate tempers should be approved by the buyer or project engineer.

Applications

  • Marine and shipbuilding fabrication
  • Welded structures and transport equipment
  • Tanks, trailers and structural panels
  • Coastal fabrication where 5083 is specified
  • Project plate and sheet requirements

Aluminium 5083 FAQs

What is Aluminium 5083 used for?

Aluminium 5083 is commonly reviewed for marine plates, sheets and welded fabrication. Final use depends on temper, dimensions, design load, corrosion exposure and buyer specification.

Which tempers are common for Aluminium 5083?

Common enquiries mention H111, H116, H321 and project-specified tempers. Buyers should confirm the exact temper required by the drawing, standard or project specification.

Which product forms can be requested in Aluminium 5083?

Buyers can send enquiries for sheets, plates, coils, bars, rods, pipes, tubes, flats, angles, channels or profiles where applicable to Aluminium 5083.

What details are needed for an Aluminium 5083 quotation?

Share grade, temper, product form, dimensions, quantity, standard, finish, inspection requirement and delivery location. Drawings are useful for machined parts or profiles.
