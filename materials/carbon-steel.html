Common Carbon Steel Grades

  • ASTM A36: structural carbon steel for plates, sections, frames and fabrication
  • SAE 1018: low carbon steel for machining, rods, bars and general engineering
  • SAE 1045: medium carbon steel for shafts, pins, gears and higher-strength parts
  • ASTM A516 Grade 70: pressure vessel plate grade for boiler, tank and equipment requirements
  • 1095 and other high carbon grades: reviewed only when buyer specification, form and quantity are clearly shared

Typical Applications

  • Structural fabrication, frames, base plates, supports and infrastructure items
  • Machined components, shafts, pins, bushes, spacers and engineering parts
  • Industrial piping, general fabrication, flanges, fittings and fasteners where specified
  • Boilers, pressure vessels, tanks and process equipment plates where A516 Grade 70 is specified
  • Maintenance, EPC, construction, fabrication and machinery procurement

Standards and Inspection Guidance

  • Common references include ASTM A36, ASTM A29/A108 for bars where applicable, ASTM A516/A516M for pressure vessel plate and buyer project specifications.
  • For pipes, tubes, fittings or flanges, state the exact ASTM, ASME, EN, DIN, IS or project standard required.
  • Equivalent references such as IS or EN grades should not be treated as automatic substitutes without buyer approval.
  • State MTC, PMI, UT, impact testing, dimensional inspection, marking and packing requirements at enquiry stage.

Carbon Steel vs Mild Steel vs Stainless Steel

Carbon steel is selected by grade and carbon/mechanical property requirement, mild steel is often used as a broader low-carbon fabrication term, and stainless steel is selected when corrosion resistance is a primary design factor. Buyers should confirm whether the RFQ requires carbon steel, mild steel, stainless steel or a specific standard such as A36, A516 Grade 70, IS 2062, EN S275, 1018 or 1045.

Carbon Steel Supply FAQs

Which carbon steel grades does Rubinox handle?

Rubinox handles carbon steel enquiries for grades such as ASTM A36, SAE 1018, SAE 1045 and ASTM A516 Grade 70. Buyers should share grade, standard, product form, dimensions, quantity and delivery location.

What is the difference between A36, 1018, 1045 and A516 Grade 70?

A36 is commonly reviewed for structural fabrication, 1018 for machining and general engineering, 1045 for higher-strength shafts and parts, and A516 Grade 70 for pressure vessel plate when specified.

Which carbon steel grade is used for structural fabrication?

ASTM A36 is commonly specified for structural plates, angles, channels, base plates and fabricated frames. Final selection should follow the drawing, design load, standard and project specification.

Which carbon steel grade is used for machining?

SAE 1018 is often reviewed for general machining and cold-finished bars, while SAE 1045 is reviewed for higher-strength machined parts. Confirm chemistry, mechanical properties, heat treatment and dimensional tolerance in the RFQ.

Which carbon steel grade is used for pressure vessel plates?

ASTM A516 Grade 70 is commonly specified for pressure vessel plate. Buyers should share plate thickness, width, length, normalized condition, impact testing and inspection requirements where applicable.

What details are needed for a carbon steel quotation?

Share grade, standard, product form, size, thickness, schedule, diameter, length, quantity, finish or condition, inspection requirement and delivery location. Drawings or BOQ files help for project enquiries.

Can buyers request MTC, inspection or cutting support?

Buyers can mention MTC, PMI, dimensional inspection, third-party inspection, cutting, marking and packing requirements in the RFQ. Documentation and processing support is reviewed where applicable and as per requirement.

Can carbon steel grades be substituted?

Equivalent or alternate grades should be used only when approved by the buyer, project engineer or end user. Mechanical properties, chemistry, standard, heat treatment and application duty should be checked first.

Can Rubinox review IS 2062 or EN S275 as carbon steel alternatives?

Rubinox can review IS 2062 or EN S275 enquiries as related structural steel references, but they should not be treated as automatic alternatives to ASTM A36, SAE 1018, SAE 1045 or A516 Grade 70. Buyers should share the governing standard, grade, mechanical requirement and approval status.
