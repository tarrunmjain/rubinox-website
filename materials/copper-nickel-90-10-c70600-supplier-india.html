What Is Copper Nickel 90/10 C70600?

Copper Nickel 90/10 C70600 is a copper-nickel grade requested for seawater, marine, condenser, heat exchanger and piping contexts where the buyer's specification calls for UNS C70600 or Cu-Ni 90/10. Final selection should follow the governing standard, exposure condition, fabrication route and inspection plan.

Copper Nickel 90/10 C70600 FAQs

What details should I send for a Copper Nickel 90/10 C70600 RFQ?

Share UNS C70600 or Cu-Ni 90/10, product form, dimensions, quantity, governing standard, testing or inspection needs, documentation requirement and delivery location so Rubinox can review the enquiry clearly.

Is Copper Nickel 90/10 the same as Copper Nickel 70/30?

No. 90/10 C70600 and 70/30 C71500 are different copper-nickel grades. Any substitution should be approved against the buyer standard, service condition and project documentation requirement.

Which Copper Nickel 90/10 product forms can be requested?

Common RFQs include pipes, tubes, U-tubes, sheets, plates, strips, coils, flanges, fittings, bars, rods and drawing-led pipe spool enquiries where specified.

Can documentation be requested with Copper Nickel 90/10 C70600?

Yes, buyers can mention MTC, PMI, inspection, testing, marking or packing needs where applicable. Support is reviewed enquiry-wise against the material and requirement.

Is Rubinox a manufacturer of Copper Nickel 90/10 C70600?

No. Rubinox Metal and Alloys is a stockist, supplier, importer, exporter and project supplier.
