ASTM A513 Mild Steel Overview

ASTM A513 is commonly reviewed for mild steel mechanical tubing, ERW tubing, fabrication tube and engineering tube requirements where dimensional tolerance and tube condition matter. Rubinox supports RFQs based on size, wall thickness, grade/type, length, quantity and documentation requirements.

Common Product Forms

  • Round mechanical tubes and ERW tubes
  • Square or rectangular mechanical tubes where specified
  • Cut-to-length tube requirements
  • Drawing-based fabrication and engineering tube enquiries

Standards and Specification Guidance

  • Confirm ASTM A513 and any required type, grade or project specification.
  • Share OD, wall thickness, length, tolerance, finish and end condition.
  • Mechanical tubing should not be substituted with structural HSS unless the buyer specification allows it.
  • Mention inspection, packing, marking and MTC requirements where applicable.

Applications

  • Mechanical tube assemblies and fabricated parts
  • Frames, guards, fixtures and equipment components
  • General engineering and fabrication tubing
  • Automotive, machinery and maintenance tube requirements
  • Cut and prepared tube enquiries

A513 Mechanical Tube Guidance

A513 is selected when the RFQ calls for mechanical tubing rather than broad structural steel. Buyers should share the exact type, dimensional tolerance, finish and end-use requirement for accurate review.

Documentation and Inspection Notes

  • State MTC and traceability requirements in the RFQ where applicable.
  • Mention PMI, dimensional inspection, impact testing or third-party inspection only when required by the project.
  • Share marking, packing, cutting and dispatch preferences before quotation.
  • Final acceptance should follow buyer, consultant or project specification.

ASTM A513 Mild Steel FAQs

What is ASTM A513 mild steel used for?

ASTM A513 is commonly reviewed for mechanical tubing, ERW tube, fabricated tube components, frames, fixtures and engineering assemblies.

Which forms can be requested in ASTM A513?

Common enquiries include round, square or rectangular mechanical tubes, ERW tubes, cut-to-length tubes and drawing-based tube requirements.

Is A513 tubing the same as A500 HSS?

No. A513 is mechanical tubing and A500 is structural hollow section material. Substitution should be approved by the buyer or project engineer.

What details are needed for an ASTM A513 quotation?

Share ASTM A513 type or grade, OD, wall thickness, shape, length, quantity, finish, tolerance, documentation requirement and delivery location.
