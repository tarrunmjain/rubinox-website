Common Phosphor Bronze Grades

  • Phosphor Bronze C510: spring grade phosphor bronze for strips, sheets and contacts
  • Phosphor Bronze C521: high-strength phosphor bronze for springs and fatigue-resistant parts
  • Phosphor Bronze C544: free-machining phosphor bronze for rods, bars and bushes

Typical Applications

  • Electrical contacts, connectors, clips and terminals
  • Springs, spring strips and fatigue-resistant components
  • Bushings, bearings, wear parts and machined components
  • Marine, corrosion-aware and industrial hardware where specified
  • Maintenance, precision engineering and project procurement

Phosphor Bronze Supply FAQs

Which phosphor bronze grades does Rubinox handle?

Rubinox handles phosphor bronze enquiries for grades such as C510, C521 and C544. Buyers should share alloy, temper, hardness, product form, dimensions, quantity, standard and delivery location.

What is the difference between C510, C521 and C544?

C510 is commonly reviewed for spring and electrical parts, C521 for higher-strength spring and fatigue applications, and C544 for free-machining rods, bushes, bearings and wear components.

Which phosphor bronze grade is used for springs?

C510 and C521 are commonly reviewed for spring, clip and contact applications. Final selection depends on temper, hardness, fatigue requirement, dimensions and buyer specification.

Which phosphor bronze grade is used for bushes and bearings?

C544 free-machining phosphor bronze is often reviewed for bushes, bearings, wear parts and machined components when the buyer specification calls for it.

What details are needed for a phosphor bronze quotation?

Share grade, temper, hardness, product form, thickness or diameter, width, length, quantity, standard, inspection requirement and delivery location. Drawings help for bushes or machined parts.

Can Rubinox support phosphor bronze sheets, strips, coils, rods and bushes?

Rubinox can review enquiries for sheets, plates, strips, coils, foils, rods, bars, wires, bushes, bearings and machined components according to buyer grade and specification.

Can buyers request MTC or inspection support for phosphor bronze?

Buyers can mention MTC, PMI, inspection, hardness, dimensional report, marking, packing or third-party inspection requirements in the RFQ. Documentation support is reviewed where applicable and as per requirement.

Can phosphor bronze grades be substituted?

Equivalent or alternate grades should be used only when approved by the buyer, project engineer or end user. Temper, hardness, fatigue, machinability and wear requirements should be checked first.

Can Rubinox review PB1, PB2, C51000 or C52100 phosphor bronze enquiries?

Yes. Rubinox can review PB1, PB2, C51000, C52100 and other phosphor bronze reference enquiries when buyers share the exact standard, grade, temper, hardness, product form, dimensions and quantity. Alternate references should not be used as automatic substitutes without buyer or project approval.
