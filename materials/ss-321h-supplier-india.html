Why Buyers Use SS 321H

  • High-carbon version of SS 321 for elevated temperature service
  • Titanium-stabilized stainless steel for welded and thermal applications
  • Used where creep strength and high-temperature performance are important
  • Suitable for boiler, heater tube, heat exchanger and pressure equipment requirements

SS 321 vs SS 321H

  • SS 321 is commonly selected for stabilized stainless steel fabrication
  • SS 321H is preferred where higher carbon content and high-temperature strength are required
  • Both grades are used in welded and thermal service applications
  • Final grade selection should follow project specification, drawing or end-user approval
