Zeron 100 / UNS S32760 Overview

Zeron 100 is a super duplex stainless steel grade commonly associated with UNS S32760. Buyers usually specify it for demanding chloride, seawater, brine, offshore and process environments where the project standard calls for this grade or trade-name requirement.

Rubinox does not treat trade names as automatic substitutes. Share the exact project specification, UNS number, standard, product form, size, quantity and documentation requirement so the enquiry can be reviewed correctly.

Common Applications

  • Seawater intake, cooling water and discharge systems.
  • Desalination, brine handling and high-pressure piping packages.
  • Offshore topside, marine and oil and gas process components.
  • Chemical processing, scrubber, heat exchanger and pressure equipment enquiries.
  • Project components where UNS S32760 is named in the specification.

Standards and Specification Guidance

  • UNS: Zeron 100 is commonly associated with UNS S32760.
  • Pipe: ASTM A790 / ASME SA790 where applicable to duplex pipe enquiries.
  • Tube: ASTM A789 / ASME SA789 where applicable.
  • Plate, sheet and strip: ASTM A240 / ASME SA240 where applicable.
  • Bars and forgings: ASTM A276, ASTM A479 or ASTM A182 depending on product and application.
  • Fittings: ASTM A815 / ASME SA815 for wrought piping fittings where specified.

Documentation and Inspection Notes

Zeron 100 project enquiries may require MTC review, PMI, hydro testing, ferrite check, corrosion testing, impact testing, third-party inspection, packing notes or project-specific documentation. Share these requirements at enquiry stage so Rubinox can review the supply route accurately.

Zeron 100 Supply FAQs

What is Zeron 100?

Zeron 100 is a super duplex stainless steel grade commonly associated with UNS S32760. Buyers should share the exact project specification, product form, size, testing and documentation scope for quotation review.

Is Zeron 100 the same as Super Duplex 2507?

Zeron 100 and Super Duplex 2507 are both super duplex stainless steel options, but they are specified differently. Zeron 100 is commonly associated with UNS S32760, while Super Duplex 2507 is commonly associated with UNS S32750. The project specification should decide the required grade.

Which product forms can be quoted in Zeron 100?

Zeron 100 enquiries may include pipes, tubes, plates, sheets, bars, rods, flanges, fittings, fasteners and project components. Availability and route should be reviewed against the exact size, quantity and specification.

Where is Zeron 100 commonly reviewed?

Zeron 100 is commonly reviewed for demanding chloride, seawater, offshore, desalination, chemical process and marine service where the project calls for UNS S32760 or a named super duplex requirement.

What standards should be shared for Zeron 100 RFQs?

Share the applicable ASTM, ASME, EN, NORSOK or project specification, along with UNS S32760, product form, size, quantity, testing requirement, delivery location and documentation scope.

Can Rubinox support Zeron 100 enquiries for export buyers?

Rubinox can review Zeron 100 and UNS S32760 enquiries for PAN India and export buyers when grade, standard, size, quantity, destination and documentation requirements are clearly shared.

What details are needed for a Zeron 100 quotation?
