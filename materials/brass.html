Brass Supply FAQs

Which brass grades does Rubinox handle?

Rubinox handles brass enquiries for grades such as C260, C360, C464, C280 and C385. Buyers should share alloy, temper, product form, dimensions, quantity, standard and delivery location.

What is the difference between C260, C360 and C464 brass?

C260 is commonly reviewed for cartridge brass sheet and formed parts, C360 for free cutting rods and machined parts, and C464 for naval brass or marine-related requirements where specified.

Which brass grade is suitable for machining?

C360 free cutting brass is commonly reviewed for machining, turned parts, fittings, inserts and precision components. The final choice should follow drawing, tolerance and buyer specification.

Which brass grade is reviewed for marine hardware?

C464 naval brass is often reviewed for marine hardware and corrosion-aware applications when the project specification calls for C464.

What details are needed for a brass quotation?

Share brass grade, temper, product form, rod diameter, sheet thickness, tube OD and wall, length, quantity, standard, finish, inspection requirement and delivery location.

Can Rubinox support brass rods, bars, sheets, plates, pipes and tubes?

Rubinox can review enquiries for brass rods, bars, sheets, plates, strips, pipes, tubes, flats, angles, circles, profiles, fittings and machined components according to buyer specifications.

Can buyers request MTC or inspection support for brass?

Buyers can mention MTC, PMI, inspection, marking, packing or third-party inspection requirements in the RFQ. Documentation support is reviewed where applicable and as per requirement.

Can brass grades be substituted?

Equivalent or alternate brass grades should be used only when approved by the buyer, project engineer or end user. Machinability, corrosion exposure, strength, temper and lead-content restrictions should be checked first.

Can Rubinox review CZ108, CZ121, CZ124 or C272 brass enquiries?

Yes. Rubinox can review CZ108, CZ121, CZ124, C272 and other brass reference enquiries when buyers share exact standard, composition, temper, product form, dimensions and quantity. These references should not be treated as automatic substitutes for C260, C360, C464, C280 or C385 unless approved by the buyer or project specification.
