Chloride Resistance Guidance

Duplex stainless steels are commonly selected when buyers need a balance of strength and chloride resistance. Duplex 2205 is often reviewed where standard austenitic grades may be insufficient, while Super Duplex 2507 and Zeron 100 are commonly reviewed for more demanding seawater, brine, offshore and high-chloride process service.

Grade choice should consider chloride concentration, temperature, pH, pressure, crevice design, fabrication, welding, cleaning chemicals and project standards. Final selection should always be checked against the actual service environment.

Seawater Applications

Duplex and Super Duplex grades are commonly reviewed for seawater intake, discharge, cooling water and marine piping packages. Super Duplex 2507 is often considered for more demanding seawater exposure, while Duplex 2205 may suit selected seawater-adjacent or less severe chloride applications.

Desalination Applications

Desalination projects may require duplex or super duplex material for reverse osmosis systems, brine handling lines, high-pressure piping, evaporators, condensers, seawater intake systems, flanges, fittings and pressure-containing components.

Offshore and Marine Applications

Offshore and marine enquiries may involve platform piping, topside components, produced water systems, marine equipment, pressure vessels and heat exchangers. Rubinox can review project documentation requirements when they are shared at enquiry stage.

Duplex and Super Duplex Supply FAQs

What is Duplex 2205 stainless steel?

Duplex 2205 is a duplex stainless steel commonly associated with UNS S32205 and S31803. It is often reviewed for strength, chloride resistance and industrial service where standard austenitic grades may not meet project requirements.

What is Super Duplex 2507 stainless steel?

Super Duplex 2507 is a higher-alloy duplex stainless steel commonly associated with UNS S32750. Buyers often consider it for seawater, desalination, offshore and high-chloride environments where stronger pitting and crevice corrosion resistance is required.

What is the difference between Duplex 2205 and Super Duplex 2507?

Duplex 2205 is widely used for strength and chloride resistance in many industrial applications, while Super Duplex 2507 is commonly reviewed for more demanding chloride and seawater service. The right grade depends on service conditions and project specifications.

What are the UNS numbers for Duplex 2205 and Super Duplex 2507?

Duplex 2205 is commonly specified as UNS S32205 or S31803. Super Duplex 2507 is commonly specified as UNS S32750, while Zeron 100 is commonly associated with UNS S32760.

Which ASTM standards apply to duplex products?

Common duplex standards include ASTM A790 for pipe, ASTM A789 for tubing, ASTM A240 for sheet and plate, ASTM A276 or A479 for bars, ASTM A182 for flanges and forged fittings and ASTM A815 for wrought fittings.

Is duplex stainless steel suitable for seawater service?

Duplex stainless steel may be suitable for selected seawater and marine service, depending on grade, chloride level, temperature, design and project standards. Super Duplex 2507 is often reviewed for more demanding seawater exposure.

Is Super Duplex 2507 suitable for desalination plants?

Super Duplex 2507 is commonly considered for desalination plants, brine handling, seawater intake, high-pressure piping and related components. Buyers should share service conditions, standards, testing and documentation requirements before final selection.

How does duplex stainless steel compare with 316L?

Duplex stainless steel generally offers higher strength and stronger chloride resistance than 316L in many applications, but 316L can still be suitable for moderate service. Selection should follow the operating environment and project specification.

How does Super Duplex compare with SMO 254?

Super Duplex 2507 and SMO 254 are both reviewed for demanding chloride service, but they belong to different stainless steel families. Super Duplex is often selected for strength plus chloride resistance, while SMO 254 is a 6Mo austenitic grade selected for corrosion-driven requirements.

What details are required for a Duplex or Super Duplex quotation?

Share grade, UNS number, ASTM or project standard, product form, size, quantity, testing requirement, service environment, delivery location and timeline. Drawings or project specifications are helpful for flanges, fittings and custom items.

Can Rubinox support test certificates and inspection requirements?

Rubinox can support enquiries where material test certificates, PMI, hydro testing, impact testing, ferrite checks, corrosion testing or third-party inspection are required. Documentation requirements should be shared clearly at enquiry stage.
