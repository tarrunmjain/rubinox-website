Titanium Grade 1 vs Grade 2 vs Grade 5 vs Grade 23

Grade Type UNS Reference Typical ASTM References Common Buyer Use
Grade 1 Commercially pure titanium UNS R50250 ASTM B265, B338, B348 and B381 as applicable Maximum formability, corrosion-resistant sheets, plates, strips and formed components.
Grade 2 Commercially pure titanium UNS R50400 ASTM B265, B338, B348, B861, B862 and B381 as applicable General-purpose corrosion resistance for chemical, marine, heat exchanger and industrial equipment.
Grade 5 Ti-6Al-4V alloy UNS R56400 ASTM B265, B348, B381 and F1472 as applicable High-strength aerospace, fasteners, machined parts, structural and performance components.
Grade 23 Ti-6Al-4V ELI UNS R56407 ASTM F136, B348 and B265 as applicable Medical, surgical-related, precision, aerospace and high-integrity applications where specified.

Final grade selection should follow the drawing, project specification, service environment, testing scope and documentation requirement. Titanium Grade 5 or Grade 23 is not automatically better than commercially pure titanium; each grade serves a different procurement need.

Titanium ASTM Specifications and UNS References

Titanium enquiries should mention the grade, UNS number, product form, ASTM or project standard, size, finish, testing and documentation requirement. Common references include:

  • ASTM B265: titanium sheet, strip and plate.
  • ASTM B338: seamless and welded titanium tubes for condensers and heat exchangers.
  • ASTM B348: titanium bars and billets.
  • ASTM B381: titanium forgings and forged fittings.
  • ASTM B861 / B862: seamless and welded titanium pipe.
  • ASTM F136 / F1472: medical or surgical-related alloy references where specified by the buyer.

Grade 1 may be reviewed when formability is the main requirement, while alloy grades are selected when strength or project standards require them.

  • Marine, seawater, desalination and coastal applications.
  • Chemical plant parts, piping, fittings and fabricated items.

Titanium Buyer Specification Guide

A complete titanium enquiry should include the details needed to identify the correct material, route, inspection scope and delivery requirement.

  • Finish, condition, machining, fabrication or packing requirement.
  • MTC, PMI, third-party inspection, dimensional report or traceability requirement.

Titanium Supply FAQs

Which titanium grades does Rubinox supply?

Rubinox can support enquiries for Titanium Grade 1, Grade 2, Grade 5 and Grade 23 in common product forms such as sheets, plates, pipes, tubes, bars, rods, flanges, fittings, fasteners and drawing-based components.

What is the difference between Titanium Grade 1 and Grade 2?

Grade 1 is commercially pure titanium with excellent formability, while Grade 2 is a widely used commercially pure grade for corrosion-resistant industrial service. The right grade depends on strength, formability, standard, size and application requirements.

How does Titanium Grade 5 compare with Grade 23?

Grade 5 is Ti-6Al-4V, a high-strength titanium alloy used for aerospace, fasteners, machined parts and performance components. Grade 23 is Ti-6Al-4V ELI and is often specified where ELI chemistry, precision or medical-related standards are required.

What is the UNS number for Titanium Grade 2?

Titanium Grade 2 is commonly associated with UNS R50400. Buyers should mention the grade, UNS reference, ASTM or project specification, product form, size and testing requirement when sending an enquiry.

Which ASTM standards should buyers mention?

Common titanium standards include ASTM B265 for sheet and plate, ASTM B338 for tubes, ASTM B348 for bars, ASTM B381 for forgings, ASTM B861 for seamless pipe and ASTM B862 for welded pipe. Medical-related alloy enquiries may specify ASTM F136 or ASTM F1472 where applicable.

Is titanium suitable for aerospace applications?

Titanium Grade 5 and selected titanium alloys are commonly reviewed for aerospace and high-performance engineering applications because of their strength-to-weight ratio and corrosion resistance. Final suitability depends on the buyer drawing, material specification, testing and documentation requirement.

Is titanium suitable for medical applications?

Titanium Grade 23 is often specified for medical, surgical-related and precision applications where the buyer defines the correct standard and certificate requirement. Rubinox does not provide regulatory certification; buyers should share the required specification and documentation scope.

Which titanium grade is commonly used in chemical processing?

Titanium Grade 2 is commonly reviewed for chemical processing, marine, heat exchanger and condenser applications due to its corrosion-resistant positioning. Grade selection should still follow service conditions, project standards, fabrication needs and inspection requirements.

What details are required for a titanium quotation?

Share the grade, UNS reference if available, ASTM or project standard, product form, size, quantity, finish, testing or certificate requirement, application, delivery location and timeline. Drawings are helpful for machined or custom components.

Can Rubinox support titanium enquiries with test certificates?

Rubinox can support enquiries where material test certificates, traceability, PMI, third-party inspection or dimensional documentation are required. Certificate and inspection requirements should be shared clearly at enquiry stage.
