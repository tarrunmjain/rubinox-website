# SEO Growth Roadmap - Phase 13

## D13A - Indexing Activation And Baseline

Prepare Search Console and Bing owner actions, create the priority URL tracker, create the 30-day monitoring dashboard and define reopening triage rules. No new pages or visible website changes.

## D13B - 7 To 14 Day Indexing Triage

Analyse Search Console and Bing exports, group indexing reasons and fix only validated issues. Do not perform broad metadata rewrites or new page generation.

## D13C - Authority Growth

Use genuine, owner-approved activity only:

- Google Business activity.
- Relevant business citations.
- Supplier and trade-directory profile consistency.
- Genuine review programme.
- Editorial and association links.
- Customer/vendor reference links only when genuine and approved.

Do not invent clients, projects, awards, reviews, approvals or certifications.

## D13D - Conversion Growth

Use GA4 and enquiry-path evidence:

- Organic landing-page analysis.
- WhatsApp, email and call CTA tracking review.
- Quotation completion analysis.
- High-traffic / low-enquiry page improvements.
- No redesign without evidence.

## D13E - Monthly Monitoring

Track indexed-page trend, query growth, CTR opportunities, ranking positions 8-20, city/material enquiry trends and technical health.

Do not create additional pages until indexing and performance data identifies a specific, validated gap.
