## Material / Grade / Form Strategy

Stainless Steel India pages should lead with SS 304, SS 316, SS 316L and city-specific forms. SS 202, SS 310, SS 410, SS 420 and SS 430 should be used only where form + city intent is strong enough.

Nickel Alloy pages should focus on Inconel 625/718, Monel 400/K500, Hastelloy C276/C22 and Nickel 200/201 where the page can explain forms, use cases, documentation and RFQ details safely.

Duplex and Super Duplex pages should focus on Duplex 2205 and Super Duplex 2507 pipes, plates, fittings, flanges, fasteners and export-country applications where corrosion-service context is useful.

Carbon Steel, HSS, Copper, Brass and Aluminium should remain a smaller controlled track unless owner increases priority. HSS should be India-cluster-specific and focused on bars, rods and flats.

## Not Recommended Candidates

- Generic all-India material supplier pages: too broad and likely to overlap existing material hubs.
- Broad city-only pages where a city hub already exists: use combo pages instead.
- Any page promising fixed prices, current rates, fixed availability or dispatch promises: unsafe and not aligned with Rubinox rules.
- False role pages using unsupported source-role language: not allowed.
- Raw certificate or registration-detail pages: not part of public SEO expansion.
- HSS T1/T42 pages: defer unless owner confirms current business scope and enough content depth.
- Export pages for very small markets without a strong industrial basis: likely thin.
- Near-duplicate pages that only swap one city name while keeping the same body copy: do not create.

## Duplicate / Cannibalization Warnings

- Existing city pages should not be duplicated with another broad "supplier in city" page.
- Existing export country pages should act as hubs; future export combo pages must target material/form/country intent.
- Existing material and grade pages should not be copied into city pages. New pages must add city/country RFQ context, forms, industries and internal links.
- Existing price-factor guide pages should remain informational; D11B should not create fixed public pricing pages.
- Similar city clusters should be grouped when the existing site already groups them, such as Chakan/PCMC, Dholera/Sanand, Sriperumbudur/Oragadam, Indore/Pithampur and Kolkata/Howrah/Durgapur.

## Content Template Recommendation

Every High-priority page must be strong enough to support:

- Clear H1 with material/grade/form/city or country intent.
- Safe Rubinox positioning as stockist, supplier, importer, exporter and project supplier.
- Buyer-focused intro without stock, price, delivery or false role claims.
- Forms and grade table.
- Industry/use-case mapping.
- RFQ checklist: grade, form, size, quantity, standard, documents and delivery location.
- Documentation note for MTC, PMI, inspection or test support where applicable.
- Safe FAQ section.
- Internal links to material, grade, form, city/country, quotation and contact pages.
- Schema recommendation: WebPage, BreadcrumbList, Organization reference, and FAQPage only when visible FAQ content exists.

Reject or downgrade a page if it cannot support these blocks without repeated copy.

## Internal Linking Strategy

- Link from existing city pages to future city-specific material/form pages.
- Link from material and grade pages to only the most relevant city/country combos.
- Link export country pages to country-specific material/form pages.
- Link product-form hub pages to form-specific combos such as sheets, plates, pipes, rods, flanges and fittings.
- Link quotation and technical resources to RFQ-heavy combo pages.
- Keep breadcrumbs shallow and stable.

## Schema Strategy

Future D11B pages should use:

- 'WebPage'.
- 'BreadcrumbList'.
- 'Organization' reference consistent with existing site.
- 'FAQPage' only when page-visible FAQ content exists.

Do not add unsupported Product availability, Offer, Review, Rating or certification markup.
