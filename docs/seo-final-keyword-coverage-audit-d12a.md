## Remaining High-Value Gaps

- Stainless Steel India: SS 316, SS 316L, SS 310, SS 410, SS 420 and SS 430 city-grade-form pages are still underrepresented compared with SS 304 fittings/flanges/pipes.
- Stainless Steel forms: sheets, coils, welded pipes, seamless pipes, tubes, round bars, fasteners, wire mesh and perforated sheets have useful gaps in major industrial clusters.
- Nickel Alloys India: Inconel 625 is present for fittings/flanges, but plates, sheets, rods and round bars remain useful in selected cities. Inconel 718, Monel 400, Hastelloy C276 and Nickel 200 have fewer city-form combinations.
- Duplex and Super Duplex: Duplex 2205 and Super Duplex 2507 need only selective expansion for pipes, plates, flanges and fittings in process, marine and chemical clusters.
- HSS India: M35 and M42 pages are missing; M2 currently has only round-bar coverage.
- Export: current export-material pages focus heavily on Inconel 625 and Duplex 2205 fittings/flanges. Useful gaps remain for Super Duplex 2507, Hastelloy C276, stainless 316 and nickel alloy plates/round bars in high-fit countries.
- Commercial metals: Aluminium 6061 plates/sheets, Copper C110 sheets/rods and selected Brass rods/sheets can support a small number of final pages, but should stay limited.

## Are 500 More Pages Recommended?

No. The current 514-page site already covers most broad keyword groups, and the remaining value is concentrated in a smaller set of clear RFQ-intent gaps.

Adding 500 more pages now is not recommended because:

- Many extra rows would be weak city swaps or country swaps.
- Internal-link quality would be diluted.
- Crawl budget and owner review effort would increase sharply.
- More pages would increase overlap risk with existing hubs, grade pages and D11B/D11C pages.
- A 3-day freeze is better served by a final focused batch plus indexing/QA.

## Recommended Final Page Count

Recommended D12B count: 120 pages.

Recommended D12B mix:

- Stainless Steel India: 72 pages (60%).
- Nickel Alloys + Duplex/Super Duplex India/export: 36 pages (30%).
- Carbon Steel, HSS and commercial Aluminium/Copper/Brass: 12 pages (10%).

This is large enough to close the strongest gaps, but small enough to keep pages content-rich and reviewable before freeze.

## What Not To Create

- Do not create 500 extra pages before the freeze.
- Do not create price/current-rate pages.
- Do not create broad duplicate pages where a hub or exact grade page already satisfies the intent.
- Do not create weak pages only by replacing a city or country name.
- Do not create public pages for unsupported approvals, client references, ratings or guaranteed commercial promises.
- Do not add low-value export pages for countries with weak industrial fit unless future demand data supports them.
- Do not add broad state pages unless the page can offer a distinct sourcing/use-case angle beyond existing city hubs.

## Risks Of Over-Expansion

- Thin or repetitive pages can weaken the overall search footprint.
- Similar titles/H1s across too many destinations can create cannibalization.
- Excessive internal links can make important hubs less readable.
- A final freeze becomes harder if hundreds of pages are added without review time.

## Quality Guardrails

- Rubinox role remains: Stockist | Supplier | Importer | Exporter | Project Supplier.
- Use safe RFQ wording only.
- Every approved D12B page needs 800+ words where practical, 4-6 FAQs, RFQ checklist, documentation note, related links and valid schema.
- No Offer, Review, Rating, availability, price or commercial-promise schema.
- No footer/nav bloat.
- No public URL changes to existing pages.
- Preserve GA4 exactly.
