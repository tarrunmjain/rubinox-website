# Indexing Triage Rules - D13A

## Purpose

These rules define when the frozen Rubinox website may be reopened after D12C. The default response is to monitor, not create pages, redesign, rewrite metadata or repeatedly resubmit the sitemap.

## Valid Reasons To Reopen

- Live URL returns a persistent error.
- Wrong noindex.
- Wrong canonical.
- Sitemap omission.
- Broken internal link.
- Entity mismatch.
- Schema error.
- Clearly duplicated generated content.
- Search Console identifies a specific technical blocker.
- Important page remains unindexed after a reasonable monitoring period and the reason is understood.
- Owner-approved factual update.

## Not Valid By Themselves

- Page has not indexed within a few days.
- Ranking is not immediate.
- Desire to add more city pages without data.
- Arbitrary SEO-tool score.
- Competitor has more pages.
- Repeated sitemap submission.
- Changing titles every few days.
- Adding more keywords to every paragraph.

## Priority Levels

### P0 Critical

Site unavailable, widespread noindex, sitemap failure, canonical-domain failure, GA4 removed or mass 404/500.

### P1 High

Important hub not indexed due to a technical issue, broken internal discovery, schema failure on a page family or duplicate canonical generator error.

### P2 Medium

Selected priority page needs content improvement based on query data, CTR improvement based on high impressions and low clicks or internal-link improvement based on indexing data.

### P3 Monitor

Newly published page not yet crawled, low impressions during the initial indexing period or normal ranking fluctuations.

## Business Guardrails

Rubinox Metal and Alloys remains positioned as Stockist | Supplier | Importer | Exporter | Project Supplier. Do not add manufacturer, factory, ready-stock, price, delivery guarantee, certification, approval, client, project, review, rating or award claims during indexing triage.
