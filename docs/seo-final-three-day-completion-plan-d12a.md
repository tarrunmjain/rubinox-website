# D12A - Three-Day Final SEO Completion Plan

## Day 1

- Finalize this D12A gap audit.
- Review `docs/seo-final-page-candidates-d12b.csv`.
- Owner approves or reduces the D12B count.
- Keep the current 514-page site live and unchanged during approval.

## Day 2

- If approved, create the final D12B page batch.
- Recommended D12B size: 120 pages.
- Preserve the 60/30/10 material mix.
- Update controlled discovery hubs, sitemap and schema only as required by the approved pages.
- Keep footer and navigation free from bulk page links.

## Day 3

- Run full local QA and live QA after deployment.
- Prepare Search Console and Bing sitemap submission notes.
- Freeze SEO page creation after D12B unless owner approves a correction or data-led follow-up.
- Record final sitemap, schema, analytics, link and design-lock status in freeze documentation.

## Completion Guardrail

Do not add 200, 300, 400 or 500 pages in one more burst unless the owner separately approves a larger D12B and the candidate matrix proves every page has unique RFQ intent, non-duplicate structure and enough content depth.
