# Location SEO Architecture - Batch 4

Date: 2026-06-29
Scope: planning and data architecture only. No public pages were created or edited.

## 1. Executive Summary

Batch 4 creates a controlled location SEO architecture for Rubinox Metal and Alloys. It maps Indian cities, industrial states and export markets to planned page types, risk controls, safe wording and implementation gates.

## 2. Why Not To Create Thousands Of Pages Immediately

Large location programs can multiply URLs faster than quality can be maintained. Rubinox should expand only where search demand, buyer utility and differentiated content are clear. Search Console impressions, RFQ patterns and owner-approved operational proof should guide implementation.

## 3. Doorway-Page Risk Explanation

Doorway risk appears when many pages repeat the same content with only a city, state or product name swapped. Future pages need genuine buyer value: industrial context, materials, product forms, standards, documentation notes, RFQ details and useful links.

## 4. Keyword-To-Primary-URL Mapping Rule

Every keyword has one primary URL in data/keyword-master-map.csv. Secondary URLs support the topic but should not compete for the same exact intent.

## 5. City Page Strategy

City pages should target broad local buyer intent such as stainless steel supplier in Pune or metal supplier in Chennai. Existing city pages remain primary where available. New pages should be created only when they can add differentiated industrial context and safe PAN India supply positioning.

## 6. State Page Strategy

State pages should be fewer than city pages. They should summarize industrial regions, top materials, product forms, city links and RFQ requirements without implying local warehouses or guaranteed logistics.

## 7. Country/Export Page Strategy

Country pages should use exporter-from-India wording. Existing export pages stay primary for broad export queries. Product-country pages should be limited to high-intent combinations backed by query or enquiry data.

## 8. Product-City Page Strategy

Product-city pages are high risk because they can become repetitive. Create them only for proven combinations. Merge close variants where possible, such as seamless and welded pipe on one SS pipe city page.

## 9. Dealer Keyword Handling Strategy

Dealer keywords are search-intent labels only. Public pages should use supplier, stockist, importer, exporter and project supplier wording. Do not imply authorized dealership, a local dealer branch or city-specific inventory.

## 10. Safe Wording Bank

- supplier support for Chennai buyers
- RFQ support for Pune buyers
- exporter from India for Dubai/UAE enquiries
- PAN India supply coordination from Mumbai
- documentation support where applicable
- MTC, PMI or third-party inspection support if specified
- supply feasibility subject to grade, size, quantity and sourcing review

## 11. Unsafe Wording Bank

- local office in an unverified city
- local branch in an unverified city
- local warehouse or stockyard
- ready stock in every city
- same-day or guaranteed delivery
- authorized dealer unless verified
- manufacturer, factory, mill or production claims
- fake certifications, client names, project names, reviews or ratings
- fixed prices, live prices, price lists or lowest-price claims

## 15. Phase 1 Page Recommendations

Improve the locations hub and create only the most useful 30-50 city pages after query review.

## 16. Phase 2 Page Recommendations

Create or refine 10-20 country/export pages using export-from-India wording. Prioritize UAE/Dubai and GCC markets first.

## 17. Phase 3 Page Recommendations

Create 10-15 industrial state pages after city data is reviewed. Start with Maharashtra, Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Telangana and Delhi NCR.

## 18. Page Template Recommendations

Future location pages should include an answer-first intro, location buyer context, top materials, product forms, industries, RFQ details, documentation notes, related links and careful FAQs.

## 19. Internal Linking Strategy

Link to the locations hub, relevant city/state/country pages, material pages, product form pages, technical resources and quotation. Avoid footer keyword stuffing and repeated exact-match link blocks.

## 20. Schema Strategy

Use WebPage and BreadcrumbList as baseline. Use FAQPage only when visible FAQ content exists. Do not add fake LocalBusiness schema for cities without real addresses. Do not add Product offers, prices or availability unless real and maintained.

## 21. QA Requirements For Future Location Pages

Before publishing any future location page, run sitemap, crawl, schema, accessibility, navigation, HTML validation, link checks and claim scans. Confirm one H1, safe metadata, canonical consistency, no horizontal overflow, working RFQ links and no unverified local presence claims.
