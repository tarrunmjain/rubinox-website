# Rubinox Next 30 Days Action Checklist

Date: 2026-07-02
Scope: owner and SEO monitoring checklist after Batch 13A trust asset launch and Batch 13B final lock.

## Operating Rule

Do not start new public-page expansion, SEO rewrite, schema change, sitemap change, H1/title/meta/canonical change or internal-link strategy change until monitoring data shows a clear need and the owner approves a focused batch.

## Day 0 To Day 2

- Confirm GA4 Realtime receives safe lead-intent events from the live website:
  - WhatsApp click.
  - Email click.
  - Phone click on mobile or mobile emulation.
  - Quotation CTA click.
  - Company profile PDF download.
  - Technical resources and quality documentation clicks.
- In Google Search Console, inspect and request indexing for the priority URL list from `docs/search-console-bing-indexing-status-batch-11d.md`.
- In Bing Webmaster Tools, submit the same priority URLs if manual submission is available.
- Confirm `https://rubinoxmetal.com/sitemap.xml` remains successful in GSC and Bing.
- Confirm the Google review QR and review link open the official review flow.
- Confirm the public company profile PDF downloads successfully.

## Day 7 Review

- Export or screenshot Google Search Console Pages status.
- Export or screenshot Google Search Console Performance for top queries, top pages, impressions, clicks and average position.
- Check whether the priority URLs moved from discovered/crawled not indexed into indexed status.
- Check Bing URL status for the same priority pages.
- Review GA4 events for WhatsApp, email, phone, quotation CTA, RFQ intent and PDF download activity.
- Log any broken external platform links, but do not change public pages unless a real broken link is confirmed.

## Day 14 Review

- Compare GSC query/page movement against the Day 7 snapshot.
- Identify pages with impressions but low CTR for possible future title/meta testing.
- Identify commercial pages still not indexed for possible future content or internal-link review.
- Review GA4 path and event data for high-intent user flows.
- Check whether company profile PDF engagement exists and whether it supports RFQ flow decisions.
- Keep location/product expansion paused unless query data and owner priority justify it.

## Day 30 Review

- Decide whether a focused SEO improvement batch is needed for pages still not indexed after 30 days.
- Decide whether title/meta testing is warranted for pages with impressions but weak CTR.
- Decide whether RFQ backend implementation should start based on real lead volume and owner process readiness.
- Decide whether additional real images are worth adding: product rack, packing/dispatch or approved contact/team photo.
- Decide whether a small data-led location, export, material or product-form expansion batch is justified.
- Prepare a monthly SEO and conversion report using `docs/monthly-seo-measurement-report-template.md`.

## Safety Reminders

- Do not add manufacturer, factory, mill, stockyard, warehouse, production, ready-stock, stock promise, fixed-price, fake certification, fake approval, client, project, testimonial, review snippet, rating or delivery guarantee claims.
- Do not publish raw certificates, private documents, bank details or vendor onboarding packs.
- Do not publish client/project references unless the owner later provides explicit public permission.
- Keep Rubinox identity as stockist, supplier, importer, exporter and project supplier.
