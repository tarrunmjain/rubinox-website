
# Rubinox Location SEO Plan

Date: 2026-06-29
Scope: planning only. No new public pages are authorized by this file.

## Principles

- Location SEO must support real buyers without implying local branches, warehouses, stockyards or offices.
- Use Mumbai/PAN India/export support positioning honestly.
- Use supplier for Chennai buyers, RFQ support for Pune buyers and exporter from India for Dubai/UAE enquiries style wording.
- Do not create product-city pages until keyword mapping and Search Console data support them.
- Do not create doorway pages.

## Recommended Sequence

1. Audit existing location pages and map them to data/location-seo-map.csv.
2. Improve the locations hub and internal links.
3. Use Search Console data to identify top city impressions and queries.
4. Prioritize 30-50 city pages only where buyer value and query intent are clear.
5. Plan 10-15 industrial state pages only after city data is reviewed.
6. Plan 10-20 country/export pages around verified export enquiry support.
7. Consider product-city pages only for proven high-intent combinations.

## Safe Claim Examples

- Stainless steel supplier support for Chennai buyers.
- RFQ support for Pune fabrication and project buyers.
- Exporter from India for Dubai/UAE enquiries.
- Documentation support may be available as per customer requirement.
- Dispatch coordination for PAN India enquiries.

## Unsafe Claims To Avoid

- Local Chennai office unless a real office exists.
- Ready stock in every city.
- Same-day delivery or guaranteed delivery timelines.
- Local warehouse, stockyard or branch without proof.
- Authorized dealer unless formally verified.
- Country-specific approvals, client references or certifications without owner proof.
