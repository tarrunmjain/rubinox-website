Material Location RFQ Pages FAQs

Are these pages intended as fixed-rate pages?

No. They support RFQ preparation and do not publish fixed commercial values.

What should I include in an RFQ?

Share material, grade, product form, size, quantity, destination, end use and document expectations.

Are all pages linked from the footer?

No. Discovery is handled through controlled hubs so the final footer remains clean.

Can export buyers use these hubs?

Yes. Export buyers should include destination, packing, marking and documentation requirements in the enquiry.
