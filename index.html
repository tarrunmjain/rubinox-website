Metal Supply FAQs for Buyers

Practical answers for buyers comparing industrial metal suppliers, preparing RFQs and checking documentation needs before sending an enquiry to Rubinox Metal and Alloys.

What materials does Rubinox Metal and Alloys supply?

Rubinox supplies stainless steel, duplex and super duplex, nickel alloys, titanium, SMO 254, aluminium, copper, brass, phosphor bronze, carbon steel, mild steel and special engineering alloys. Enquiries can include pipes, tubes, sheets, plates, coils, flanges, fittings, fasteners, bars, rods and project-specific product forms.

Is Rubinox a manufacturer or supplier?

Rubinox Metal and Alloys is a stockist, supplier, importer, exporter and project supplier. It should not be described as a manufacturer. The company supports buyers by reviewing material grade, product form, specification, documentation and supply requirements through suitable sourcing and project enquiry coordination.

What details should I share for a metal quotation?

For a clear quotation review, share material, grade, product form, size, thickness, OD, schedule, class, dimensions, quantity, standard, MTC or inspection requirement and delivery city or export destination. Drawings, BOQ, MTO or project specifications can also help clarify technical requirements.

Can Rubinox support PAN India enquiries?

Yes. Rubinox is based in Mumbai and supports enquiries across India for industrial buyers, EPC teams, fabricators, purchase managers and project procurement teams. Enquiries from regions such as Mumbai, Pune, Ahmedabad, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Delhi NCR and Kolkata can be reviewed based on requirement.

Does Rubinox handle export enquiries?

Rubinox can review export enquiries for industrial metals where applicable. Buyers should share destination country, port, packing requirements, material grade, product form, quantity, standards and documentation expectations. Export documentation support can be discussed based on material route, buyer specification and shipment requirement.

Can buyers request MTC, PMI or third-party inspection?

Buyers can mention MTC, PMI or third-party inspection requirements during RFQ. Documentation and inspection support are reviewed where applicable and as per requirement. Certificates, heat number traceability and inspection scope depend on grade, product form, source route, standard and buyer specification.

Which industries does Rubinox serve?

Rubinox serves industrial metal enquiries for pharma, food processing, chemical, oil and gas, EPC projects, water treatment, marine and offshore, power generation, engineering and fabrication, infrastructure and related sectors. Material recommendations depend on application environment, grade, product form and documentation requirement.

How can I send an RFQ to Rubinox?

You can send an RFQ through WhatsApp, email or the quotation page. Include material, grade, product form, dimensions, quantity, standard, testing or documentation requirement and delivery location. For project enquiries, attach BOQ, MTO, drawings or technical specifications where available.
