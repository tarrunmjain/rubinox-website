### 5. Chloride Resistance Guidance

Recommended section title:

`Chloride Resistance Guidance for Duplex Stainless Steel`

Recommended topics:

- Duplex stainless steels combine strength and chloride stress corrosion cracking resistance in many environments.
- Duplex 2205 is commonly considered where standard austenitic grades may be insufficient.
- Super Duplex 2507 and Zeron 100 are commonly reviewed for more demanding chloride, seawater and offshore service.
- Grade choice depends on chloride concentration, temperature, pH, pressure, crevice design, fabrication, welding, cleaning chemicals and project standards.

Guardrails:

- Do not say any grade is corrosion-proof.
- Do not imply one grade is universally suitable for all chloride service.

Expected score lift: +4 to +6 points.
