## Benchmark

The Nickel Alloys page should follow the successful pattern used for SMO 254, Titanium, and Duplex:

- Expand the page from a short category overview into a practical buyer-focused landing page.
- Add comparison tables that help buyers shortlist the right alloy family and grade.

- Add industry application sections for search intent and RFQ confidence.
- Add a visible FAQ section before FAQPage schema.
- Add a buyer specification guide and RFQ checklist to improve lead quality.

## Objective

Upgrade the Nickel Alloys category page into a high-intent supplier and buyer education page for Inconel, Monel, Hastelloy, Incoloy, Nickel 200, and Nickel 201 products.

The page should help buyers answer:

- Which nickel alloy family is suitable for high temperature, corrosion, marine, chemical, or heat exchanger service?
- What is the difference between Inconel, Monel, and Hastelloy?
- When should Nickel 200 be reviewed instead of Nickel 201?
- Which UNS and ASTM references should be included in an RFQ?
- Which product forms and documents should the buyer mention for a faster quote?
- How can a buyer contact Rubinox quickly without needing pricing claims on the page?

### 2. Nickel 200 vs Nickel 201 Comparison

Purpose:

- Improve pure nickel search coverage.
- Clarify the important low-carbon distinction.
- Link naturally to the focused Nickel 200 and Nickel 201 pages.

Recommended table:

| Grade | UNS reference | Type | Typical ASTM references | Common buyer use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nickel 200 | N02200 | Commercially pure nickel | ASTM B160, B161, B162, B163, B564 | Caustic handling, electrical/electronic components, chemical processing, sheets, plates, bars, pipes and tubes |
| Nickel 201 | N02201 | Low-carbon commercially pure nickel | ASTM B160, B161, B162, B163, B564 | Similar service to Nickel 200 where lower carbon is specified, especially for elevated-temperature exposure |

Recommended buyer-safe wording:

- Nickel 200 and Nickel 201 are closely related commercially pure nickel grades.
- Nickel 201 is the low-carbon version and is often reviewed when elevated-temperature exposure makes carbon control important.
- Buyers should mention the required UNS number, ASTM standard, product form, size, quantity, MTC requirement, and end use.

Internal links:

- `/materials/nickel-200-supplier-india.html`
- `/materials/nickel-201-supplier-india.html`

Recommended coverage:
