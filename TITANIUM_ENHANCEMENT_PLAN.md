## Recommended Page Additions

### 1. Titanium Grade Comparison

Recommended section title:

`Titanium Grade 1 vs Grade 2 vs Grade 5 vs Grade 23`

Recommended table:

| Grade | Type | UNS reference | Typical ASTM references | Common buyer use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grade 1 | Commercially pure titanium | UNS R50250 | ASTM B265, ASTM B338, ASTM B348, ASTM B381 as applicable | Maximum formability, corrosion-resistant sheets, plates, strips and formed components |
| Grade 2 | Commercially pure titanium | UNS R50400 | ASTM B265, ASTM B338, ASTM B348, ASTM B861, ASTM B862, ASTM B381 as applicable | General-purpose corrosion resistance, chemical processing, marine, heat exchangers and industrial equipment |
| Grade 5 | Ti-6Al-4V alloy | UNS R56400 | ASTM B265, ASTM B348, ASTM B381, ASTM F1472 as applicable | High-strength aerospace, fasteners, machined parts, structural and performance components |
| Grade 23 | Ti-6Al-4V ELI | UNS R56407 | ASTM F136, ASTM B348, ASTM B265 as applicable | Medical, surgical, implant-related, precision, aerospace and high-integrity applications |

Content guardrails:

- Do not claim one grade is always better.
- Use wording such as `commonly selected`, `often specified`, `depends on project standards`, and `confirm final grade selection with the project specification`.
- Avoid unsupported medical implant claims unless clearly tied to buyer-specified standards and documentation.

Expected score lift: +5 to +7 points.

### 2. ASTM Specifications and UNS References

Recommended section title:

`Titanium ASTM Specifications and UNS References`

Recommended content:

- Explain that buyers should specify grade, product form, ASTM/ASME standard, size, finish, testing, certificate requirement and end use.
- Map common standards to product forms:
  - `ASTM B265`: titanium sheet, strip and plate.
  - `ASTM B338`: seamless and welded titanium tubes for condensers and heat exchangers.
  - `ASTM B348`: titanium bars and billets.
  - `ASTM B381`: titanium forgings and forged fittings.
  - `ASTM B861`: seamless titanium pipe.
  - `ASTM B862`: welded titanium pipe.
  - `ASTM F136`: wrought Ti-6Al-4V ELI for surgical implant applications where specified.
  - `ASTM F1472`: Ti-6Al-4V alloy for surgical implant applications where specified.
- Include UNS references:
  - Grade 1: `UNS R50250`.
  - Grade 2: `UNS R50400`.
  - Grade 5: `UNS R56400`.
  - Grade 23: `UNS R56407`.

Expected score lift: +4 to +6 points.

### 7. RFQ Checklist

Recommended section title:

`Request a Titanium Quotation`

Recommended checklist:

- Grade: Titanium Grade 1, Grade 2, Grade 5, Grade 23 or project equivalent.
- UNS reference, if specified.
- ASTM/ASME/EN/project specification.
- Product form: pipe, tube, sheet, plate, bar, rod, flange, fitting, fastener or machined component.
- Size details: OD, wall thickness, schedule, diameter, width, length, thickness, class or drawing.
- Quantity and unit.
- Finish, condition or fabrication requirement.
- Testing, MTC, PMI, inspection or documentation requirement.
- Application: aerospace, medical, chemical, marine, heat exchanger or general industrial.
- Delivery location and timeline.

CTA structure:

- Primary CTA: WhatsApp Quote.
- Secondary CTA: Email RFQ.
- Tertiary CTA: Quotation Page.
- Optional: Contact Rubinox.

Avoid:

- Pricing claims.
- Unsupported ready-stock promises.
- Over-promising urgent delivery.

Expected score lift: +2 to +4 points.
