### 2. 316L vs 904L vs SMO 254 Comparison

Goal:

- Capture comparison searches and help buyers justify grade selection.

Recommended section title:

- `316L vs 904L vs SMO 254: Which Grade Should You Choose?`

Recommended comparison table:

| Factor | SS 316L | SS 904L | SMO 254 / UNS S31254 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Corrosion level | Good general corrosion resistance | Better acid and chloride resistance than 316L | Very high chloride and pitting resistance |
| Molybdenum level | Lower | Higher than 316L | Very high, 6Mo family |
| Seawater suitability | Limited, depends on exposure | Better than 316L | Strong candidate for seawater and high-chloride service |
| Typical use | General process, food, fabrication | Chemical, acid, coastal service | Desalination, marine, offshore, chloride-rich process service |
| Cost positioning | Lower | Higher | Premium, application-driven selection |
| Buyer decision | Use when conditions are moderate | Use when 316L is not enough | Use when chloride/pitting risk is critical |

Important content guardrail:

- Avoid absolute claims such as "always better" or "guaranteed corrosion-proof."
- Use language like "often selected," "commonly considered," and "depends on service conditions."

Expected score lift:

- Comparison intent and buyer education: weak to strong.
- Target contribution: +4 to +6 points.

### 3. Chloride Resistance Section

Goal:

- Build topical authority around the main reason buyers search for SMO 254.

Recommended section title:

- `Why SMO 254 Is Used for Chloride-Rich Service`

Recommended points to cover:

- SMO 254 is a high-alloy austenitic stainless steel commonly associated with UNS S31254 and 6Mo stainless steel.
- It is selected where chloride exposure increases pitting and crevice corrosion risk.
- Typical environments include seawater, brackish water, process water, coastal installations, chemical plants, scrubbers, and desalination systems.
- Buyers should confirm exact grade, specification, testing, and service conditions before final selection.

Keywords to include naturally:

- `chloride resistance`
- `pitting resistance`
- `crevice corrosion`
- `seawater service`
- `high chloride environment`
- `6Mo stainless steel`
- `UNS S31254 supplier`

Expected score lift:

- Topical depth: moderate to strong.
- Target contribution: +3 to +5 points.
