How We Review Your RFQ

We first confirm the material designation, product form, dimensions, quantity and required standard. Availability may vary by grade, thickness, diameter, schedule, temper, finish and manufacturing route. If an equivalent grade or alternate delivery condition is acceptable, mention it clearly.

What Affects the Quoted Price?

Pricing depends on raw-material markets, ordered quantity, cutting or machining, testing, certification, packing, freight and delivery schedule. A complete enquiry helps us avoid assumptions and return a more accurate commercial offer.

Materials You Can Enquire For

Send requirements for stainless steel, Inconel, Monel, Hastelloy and Incoloy, duplex and super duplex, titanium, aluminium, copper, brass, carbon steel, mild steel, phosphor bronze and other specialty metals.

For Pipes, Tubes & Fittings

Include NB or OD, wall thickness or schedule, seamless or welded construction, end type, length, quantity and specification. For fittings and flanges, include type, class or pressure rating, facing and dimensional standard.

For Sheets, Plates & Bars

Include thickness or diameter, width, length, finish or condition, tolerance, quantity and whether cutting is required. For machined parts, attach a readable drawing with material and inspection notes.

Delivery, Packing & Commercial Terms

State whether pricing is required ex-works, delivered to site or for export shipment. Heavy plates, long pipes, precision-finished products and export consignments may need special packing or handling. Mention the destination postcode or port, required delivery date, preferred transport mode and any site unloading limitations. If the enquiry is part of a tender, also share the quotation deadline, validity requirement, payment terms and documentation format.

Quotation FAQs

Can I send a photograph or handwritten list?
Yes, but a typed item list or drawing reduces ambiguity and helps us respond faster.

Do you quote small and bulk quantities?
Requirements are reviewed according to grade, size, stock position and minimum supply quantity.

Can you quote delivery outside India?
Yes. Include the destination country, port or delivery terms so export packing and logistics can be considered.
