Faster RFQ Review Starts With Complete Specifications

Complete technical inputs help Rubinox review industrial metal enquiries with fewer follow-up questions.

  • Confirm material family, grade, standard and product form.
  • Share dimensions, quantity, finish, condition and delivery location.
  • Mention MTC, PMI, inspection or project documentation requirements where applicable.
  • Attach BOQ, MTO, drawings or item schedules for multi-line enquiries.

Details to Share for a Clear Metal Quotation

Use these fields to help Rubinox review your enquiry accurately without adding pricing, stock commitments or unsupported assumptions.

01

Material Family

Stainless steel, duplex, nickel alloy, titanium, SMO 254, aluminium, copper, brass or special engineering alloy.

02

Grade / Specification

Share the exact grade, UNS, ASTM, EN, DIN or project specification when known.

03

Product Form

Pipes, tubes, sheets, plates, coils, bars, rods, flanges, fittings, fasteners or drawing-based components.

04

Size / Dimensions

Include OD, ID, thickness, width, length, schedule, class, thread, bore or drawing dimensions as relevant.

05

Quantity

Mention pieces, meters, kilograms, tons, sheets, coils, sets or line-item quantities.

06

Standard

Include ASTM, ASME, EN, DIN, JIS, IS, project standard or client specification where applicable.

07

Finish / Condition

Specify annealed, pickled, polished, bright, hot rolled, cold rolled, solution annealed or other condition.

08

MTC / PMI / Inspection

Note mill test certificate, PMI, dimensional inspection, third-party inspection or other review needs.

09

Delivery Location

Share delivery city, state, project site, port, export destination or logistics preference.

10

Timeline

Mention required quotation deadline, dispatch expectation or project milestone without assuming availability.

Industrial Metal RFQ Questions

These answers help procurement teams prepare clearer metal enquiries for Rubinox review.

What details should I send for a metal quotation?

Send the material family, grade, product form, size, quantity, standard, finish or condition, documentation requirements and delivery location.

Can I send a BOQ, MTO or drawing?

Yes. Rubinox can review BOQ, MTO, drawing-based and multi-line enquiries when the item descriptions and specifications are clear.

Which materials can I enquire for?

You can enquire for stainless steel, duplex and super duplex, nickel alloys, titanium, SMO 254, aluminium, copper, brass, carbon steel, mild steel, phosphor bronze and special engineering alloys.

What product forms can Rubinox review?

Rubinox can review enquiries for pipes, tubes, sheets, plates, coils, bars, rods, flanges, fittings, fasteners and drawing-based components.

Can I request MTC, PMI or inspection?

Yes. Mention MTC, PMI, dimensional inspection, third-party inspection or testing requirements in the RFQ where applicable to the material and standard.

Can Rubinox support PAN India enquiries?

Rubinox can review PAN India enquiries for industrial buyers and project teams when delivery city, quantity and specifications are shared.

Can Rubinox support export enquiries?

Rubinox can review export enquiries where applicable. Share destination country, port, packing expectation, documentation requirements and product specifications.

Can I send photos or item lists?

Yes. Photos, item lists and sketches can help initial review, but final quotation should be based on grade, dimensions, standard and quantity.

Why are grade and dimensions important?

Grade, dimensions and standards affect material suitability, sourcing review, documentation and the accuracy of the enquiry response.

Is Rubinox a manufacturer?

No. Rubinox Metal and Alloys is a stockist, supplier, importer, exporter and project supplier for industrial metals and alloys.
