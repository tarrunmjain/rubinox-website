Procurement & Project Support

A clear RFQ helps Rubinox review the requirement faster and respond with suitable quotation support.

1

Share material, grade and form

Mention the metal family, grade or UNS reference, and product form such as pipe, plate, bar, fitting or fastener.

2

Add size, quantity and delivery/export location

Include dimensions, thickness, schedule, class, quantity and city or export destination.

3

Mention standards, MTC, PMI or inspection needs

Share ASTM, ASME, EN, DIN, JIS, IS or project specifications and any documentation requirements.

4

Receive quotation support based on requirement

Rubinox reviews the enquiry details and supports the quotation process based on the shared requirement.

About Rubinox FAQ

What does Rubinox Metal and Alloys do?

Rubinox Metal and Alloys supports industrial metal enquiries as a stockist, supplier, importer, exporter and project supplier for stainless steel, nickel alloys and related metals.

Is Rubinox Metal and Alloys a manufacturer?

No. Rubinox Metal and Alloys should be described as a stockist, supplier, importer, exporter and project supplier, not as a manufacturer.

Where is Rubinox Metal and Alloys located?

Rubinox Metal and Alloys is located at A-206, 2nd floor, C.S. No. 2251 to 2255, Krishvi Escape, Sitaram Poddar Marg, near Vinay Hotel, Kalbadevi, Mumbai - 400002, Maharashtra, India.

What materials does Rubinox supply?

Rubinox supports enquiries for stainless steel, duplex and super duplex, nickel alloys, titanium, SMO 254, aluminium, copper, brass, carbon steel, mild steel, phosphor bronze and special engineering alloys.

What product forms can buyers enquire for?

Buyers can enquire for pipes, tubes, sheets, plates, coils, bars, rods, flanges, fittings, fasteners, angles, channels, flats, circles, wire mesh and project-specific forms.

Can Rubinox support PAN India enquiries?

Yes. Rubinox is based in Mumbai and can review PAN India enquiries for industrial buyers, project teams, fabricators, EPC companies and purchase departments.

Does Rubinox handle export enquiries?

Rubinox can review export enquiries where applicable. Buyers should share destination, packing expectation, documentation requirement, material grade, product form and quantity.

Can buyers request MTC, PMI or third-party inspection support?

Yes. Buyers can mention MTC, PMI or third-party inspection requirements during RFQ. Documentation and inspection support can be reviewed where applicable.

Which industries does Rubinox serve?

Rubinox serves pharma, food processing, chemical, oil and gas, EPC projects, water treatment, marine and offshore, power generation, engineering and fabrication, infrastructure and related sectors.

How is Bharat Metals connected with Rubinox?

Rubinox Metal and Alloys works with a procurement-focused approach shaped by experience connected with Bharat Metals, its sister concern. Buyers can share material, grade, form, quantity, documentation and delivery requirements for review.
