## 1. Page Objective

### Why The Page Exists

The About Us page should give industrial buyers a clear, trustworthy company profile for Rubinox Metal and Alloys. It should explain who Rubinox is, what kinds of metal enquiries it supports, how it helps procurement teams, and why buyers can confidently contact Rubinox for RFQ review.

The page should not behave like a generic brochure page. It should function as a trust and conversion page for B2B buyers who want to know whether Rubinox is the right supply partner before sending an enquiry.

### Buyer Trust Goals

The page should build trust by communicating:
- Rubinox is a Mumbai-based `Stockist | Supplier | Importer | Exporter | Project Supplier`.
- Rubinox supports stainless steel, duplex, super duplex, nickel alloys, titanium, SMO 254 and industrial metal enquiries.
- Rubinox supports RFQ review, documentation discussion and project supply coordination.
- Rubinox can review PAN India and export enquiries where applicable.
- Rubinox is not a manufacturer and should not be represented as one.
- Bharat Metals heritage/sister concern context may be mentioned only with verified wording.

### Conversion Goals

Primary conversion goals:
- Encourage buyers to send a well-structured RFQ.
- Move users to WhatsApp, email or `/quotation.html`.
- Reinforce confidence before enquiry submission.

Secondary conversion goals:
- Link users to key material category pages.
- Link users to `/industries.html` for industry-specific context.
- Reduce uncertainty around documentation, MTC, PMI, inspection and export enquiry support.

## 6. Bharat Metals Heritage Section

### Purpose

This section should carefully explain Bharat Metals as a sister concern / heritage reference, while avoiding unsupported legal or historical claims.

### Conservative Wording Option

`Rubinox Metal and Alloys works with a procurement-focused approach shaped by experience connected with Bharat Metals, its sister concern. Buyers can share material, grade, form, quantity, documentation and delivery requirements for review.`

Pros:
- Safest wording.
- Avoids specific years, achievements or legal claims.
- Uses `sister concern` without overstating legacy.

Cons:
- Less emotionally strong.

### Moderate Wording Option

`Rubinox Metal and Alloys carries forward industrial metal market familiarity associated with Bharat Metals, its sister concern, while focusing on modern RFQ review, project supply coordination and domestic/export metal enquiries.`

Pros:
- Stronger trust signal.
- Still avoids years, client claims and certifications.

Cons:
- `carries forward` should be approved by the business owner.

### Strong Wording Option

`Built on the Bharat Metals legacy, Rubinox Metal and Alloys brings trusted metal market experience to stainless steel, nickel alloy, duplex, titanium and project supply enquiries across India and export markets.`

Pros:
- Strongest brand storytelling.

Cons:
- Higher legal/content risk.
- `legacy` and `trusted experience` should be verified.
- Avoid unless business owner confirms the exact relationship and wording.

### Legal / Content Risks

Risks to avoid:
- Years of experience unless verified.
- Founder story unless verified.
- Client names unless verified.
- Certification or approval language unless verified.
- Any implication that Bharat Metals and Rubinox are the same legal entity unless confirmed.

### Recommended Safest Version

Use the conservative version for V1 implementation:

`Rubinox Metal and Alloys works with a procurement-focused approach shaped by experience connected with Bharat Metals, its sister concern. Buyers can share material, grade, form, quantity, documentation and delivery requirements for review.`

- Bharat Metals wording if too long.

Mitigation:
- Keep cards compact.
- Use short headings.
- Use section intros sparingly.
- Place deeper industry details behind `/industries.html`.

## 16. Accessibility Notes

Accessibility requirements:
- One H1 only.
- Use semantic `<main>`, `<section>`, `<nav>` and headings in logical order.
- Decorative icons should use `aria-hidden="true"`.
- Image cards need meaningful `alt` text or accessible `role="img"` labels only where valid.
- CTA link text must be descriptive.
- Do not use empty links.
- Ensure focus states remain visible.
- Avoid using `aria-label` on elements where the validator rejects it.
- Keep color contrast strong on navy backgrounds and gold accents.

Recommended heading order:
- H1 for page title.
- H2 for major sections.
- H3 for cards, FAQ questions and sub-panels.

Keyboard / screen reader notes:
- Do not make non-clickable cards look like links unless the whole card is actually a link.
- If material cards are links, ensure each card has clear accessible text.
- If icons are decorative, hide them from assistive tech.
