Product Portfolio FAQs

What materials does Rubinox list in its portfolio?

The portfolio covers stainless steel, duplex, nickel alloys, titanium, SMO 254, aluminium, copper, brass, carbon steel, mild steel, phosphor bronze and high speed steel.

Are product forms linked to existing pages?

Yes. Product form links in this hub point to existing Rubinox product-form pages where available.

Can buyers use this page for RFQ preparation?

Yes. Buyers can use the material, form and grade links to prepare clearer RFQ details before contacting Rubinox.

Does this page confirm stock availability?

No. It is a portfolio navigation page. Buyers should send RFQ details for review based on grade, form, size and quantity.

How should buyers send an RFQ to Rubinox?

Buyers should share material, grade or specification, product form, size, quantity, delivery location and documentation requirements so the enquiry can be reviewed clearly.

Can Rubinox support PAN India and export enquiries?

Rubinox can review PAN India and export enquiries with procurement and documentation details shared by the buyer, subject to material, grade, form and sourcing feasibility.
