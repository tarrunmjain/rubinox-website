Contact and RFQ FAQs

How can I contact Rubinox Metal and Alloys?

You can contact Rubinox Metal and Alloys by phone, WhatsApp, email or by using the Mumbai office details listed on this page for RFQs and procurement support.

What details should I send for a metal quotation?

Share material family, grade or specification, product form, size, quantity, standard, documentation requirement and delivery location for a clearer quotation review.

Can I send a BOQ, MTO or drawing?

Yes. Rubinox can review BOQ, MTO, drawing-based and multi-line enquiries when item descriptions, grades, quantities and specifications are clear.

Can Rubinox support PAN India enquiries?

Yes. Rubinox is based in Mumbai and can review PAN India enquiries for industrial buyers, EPC teams, fabricators and project procurement teams.

Can Rubinox support export enquiries?

Rubinox can review export enquiries where applicable. Buyers should share destination country, port, packing expectations, documentation requirements, material grade, product form and quantity.

Can buyers request MTC, PMI or third-party inspection?

Buyers can mention MTC, PMI, dimensional inspection or third-party inspection requirements during RFQ. Documentation and inspection support is reviewed where applicable and as per requirement.

Which materials can I enquire for?

Buyers can enquire for stainless steel, duplex and super duplex, nickel alloys, titanium, SMO 254, aluminium, copper, brass, carbon steel, mild steel, phosphor bronze and special engineering alloys.

Which product forms can buyers enquire for?

Buyers can enquire for pipes, tubes, sheets, plates, coils, bars, rods, flanges, fittings, fasteners, angles, flats, circles, wire mesh and project-specific forms.

Is Rubinox a manufacturer?

No. Rubinox Metal and Alloys is a stockist, supplier, importer, exporter and project supplier for industrial metals and alloys.

Can I visit the Rubinox office?

Buyers may use the Mumbai office address listed on this page. It is recommended to call or message before visiting to confirm availability.
