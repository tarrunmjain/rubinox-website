RFQ Checklist for Industry Buyers

Clear RFQ inputs help Rubinox review grade, product form, documentation and logistics scope without implying stock availability or fixed delivery timing.

  • Industry / end usePlant, equipment, service condition or project context.
  • Material gradeASTM, ASME, API, EN, DIN, IS or customer specification.
  • Product formPipe, tube, plate, sheet, bar, flange, fitting or fastener.
  • Size detailsOD, thickness, schedule, class, length, finish or drawing.
  • QuantityItem-wise quantity, BOQ or MTO line list.
  • DocumentationMTC, PMI, inspection, packing and traceability needs where applicable.
  • DestinationCity, site, port, country and logistics notes.
  • Project notesUrgency, phase priority, alternates permitted and approvals needed by the buyer.

Industry FAQ

What details should an industry RFQ include?

Share the material family, grade or specification, product form, size, quantity, surface or end requirement, documentation needs, and delivery location.

Can Rubinox support multi-grade project enquiries?

Rubinox can coordinate quotation support for multi-grade and multi-form enquiries across stainless steel, duplex, nickel alloy, titanium, aluminium, copper, brass, carbon steel, and related materials.

Are documentation and inspection requirements available?

Documentation, test certificates, third-party inspection, packing notes, and export paperwork can be discussed where applicable and available on request.

Which sectors commonly request corrosion-resistant alloys?

Chemical processing, marine service, desalination, oil and gas, pharmaceutical utilities, and process equipment buyers often compare stainless steel, duplex, super duplex, and nickel alloy routes.

Does Rubinox support PAN India and export enquiries?

Rubinox supports PAN India supply coordination and export enquiry assistance for buyers who share specification, quantity, destination, and documentation requirements.
