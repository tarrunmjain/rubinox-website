Industries We Serve

Rubinox Metal and Alloys supplies stainless steel, duplex, super duplex, nickel alloys, 254 SMO, titanium and industrial metals for critical industrial applications across India and global markets.

Oil & Gas Industry

Duplex 2205, Super Duplex 2507, Inconel 625, Hastelloy C276 and corrosion-resistant alloys for offshore platforms, pipelines, refineries and high-pressure systems.

Chemical & Petrochemical

Nickel alloys, SMO 254 and high-performance stainless steels suitable for aggressive chemical environments and process equipment.

Marine & Shipbuilding

Super duplex stainless steel, copper alloys and corrosion-resistant materials for marine structures and seawater applications.

EPC & Infrastructure Projects

Bulk supply of structural steel, stainless steel, plates, pipes and fittings for large-scale industrial and infrastructure projects.

Fabrication & Engineering Units

Pipes, tubes, sheets, plates, flanges, fittings and fasteners for custom fabrication and engineering applications.

Power & Energy Sector

Heat-resistant and high-strength alloys for thermal, nuclear and renewable energy applications.

Materials Selected for Industrial Service

Material selection depends on pressure, temperature, corrosion exposure, fabrication method and the applicable project standard. Common enquiries include stainless steel for general process equipment, Duplex 2205 and Super Duplex 2507 for chloride service, nickel alloys for severe corrosion or elevated temperatures, and titanium for lightweight, seawater and chemical applications.

Project Products We Supply

Pipes, tubes, sheets, plates, coils, round bars, rods, flanges, fittings, fasteners, flats, angles and fabricated components can be supplied according to grade, dimensions and quantity. For project requirements, share the bill of materials or item-wise enquiry so availability can be checked accurately.

Standards & Documentation

Requirements can be reviewed against ASTM, ASME, EN, DIN and customer specifications. Material Test Certificates, heat-number traceability, inspection requirements, packing instructions and export documentation should be stated at enquiry stage.

Bulk & Scheduled Supply

Rubinox supports one-time purchases, shutdown requirements, fabrication orders and phased project dispatches. Clearly mention required delivery dates, partial-delivery acceptance and the project location when requesting a commercial offer.

What to Include in an Enquiry

Send material grade, product form, size, schedule or thickness, quantity, standard, testing requirement and delivery location. Drawings and end-user specifications are especially important for machined, forged or fabricated parts.

From Enquiry to Project Dispatch

For industrial orders, the enquiry is reviewed item by item for grade, form, size, specification and quantity. Commercial offers can account for cutting, inspection, certification, packing and freight when these requirements are stated in advance. After order confirmation, dispatch planning should identify priority items, delivery sequence and any site restrictions. This approach is especially useful for EPC packages, fabrication schedules, shutdown maintenance and multi-location projects where incomplete specifications can delay procurement.
