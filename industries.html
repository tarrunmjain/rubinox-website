Project Supply Timeline

For industrial orders, the enquiry is reviewed item by item for grade, form, size, specification and quantity before commercial and dispatch planning. Rubinox supports one-time purchases, shutdown requirements, fabrication orders and phased project dispatches when delivery schedules are stated clearly.

1. RFQ Review

BOQ, MTO, drawings, grade, quantity and delivery location are checked.

2. Material Mapping

Product forms, standards, dimensions and alternates are reviewed where permitted.

3. Documentation Scope

MTC, heat-number traceability, inspection and packing needs are confirmed.

4. Commercial Offer

Item-wise quotation, delivery planning and freight inputs are prepared.

5. Dispatch Planning

Priority items, phased supply and site restrictions are aligned before dispatch.

Documentation and Standards

Requirements can be reviewed against ASTM, ASME, EN, DIN and customer specifications. Material Test Certificates, heat-number traceability, third-party inspection requirements, packing instructions and export documentation should be stated at enquiry stage.

Project Products We Supply

Pipes, tubes, sheets, plates, coils, round bars, rods, flanges, fittings, fasteners, flats, angles and fabricated components can be supplied according to grade, dimensions and quantity.

What to Include in an Enquiry

Send material grade, product form, size, schedule or thickness, quantity, standard, testing requirement and delivery location. Drawings and end-user specifications are especially important for machined, forged or fabricated parts.

Industry FAQ

Which metals are used in pharma and food processing?

SS 304, SS 316 and SS 316L are commonly reviewed for hygienic equipment, tubes, fittings, sheets and process components, depending on finish, cleaning conditions and project specification.

Which alloys are used for chemical processing?

Chemical processing enquiries often involve stainless steel, 904L, SMO 254, Duplex, Nickel Alloys, Hastelloy and Titanium depending on media, concentration, temperature and pressure.

Which materials are reviewed for marine and offshore service?

Marine and offshore enquiries often review Duplex, Super Duplex, SMO 254, Monel, Nickel Alloys and copper alloys. Buyers should share seawater conditions, chloride exposure and project standards.

Can Rubinox support multi-item EPC project enquiries?

Yes. Send the complete bill of materials or item list with grade, size, product form, quantity, standard, testing requirement and delivery location for item-wise review.

Can third-party inspection be reviewed?

Inspection and documentation requirements can be considered when they are specified before quotation and order confirmation.

Does Rubinox supply outside Mumbai?

Yes. Rubinox supports PAN India supply, and export enquiries can be reviewed when destination, packing, documentation and delivery terms are shared.
