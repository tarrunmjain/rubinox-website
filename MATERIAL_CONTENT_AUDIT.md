# Material Content Audit

Created: 2026-06-23

Scope: category-level material pages only. No website files were modified while creating this audit.

## Scoring Method

SEO completeness score is a directional 0-100 score based on:

- Title, meta description, and H1 quality.
- Word count and depth of buyer-focused copy.
- Internal links to grade, product, related material, quotation, and contact pages.
- Product forms, grades, and industries covered.
- FAQ, schema, and CTA coverage.
- Lead-generation readiness for RFQ-style buyers.

Classification:

- Complete: 85-100.
- Partially Complete: 70-84.
- Needs Major Work: below 70.

## Cross-Site Recommendations

- Add FAQ sections to Stainless Steel, Duplex & Super Duplex, Nickel Alloys, Titanium, and SMO 254.

- Add RFQ checklist blocks consistently: grade, form, standard, size, quantity, testing, delivery location, timeline.
- Add more industry-specific copy where thin: brass, phosphor bronze, special engineering alloys.
- Keep WebPage and BreadcrumbList schema; consider FAQPage schema only after adding visible FAQ content.
- Avoid changing complete pages before closing the biggest lead-generation gaps on SMO 254, Titanium, Duplex/Super Duplex, and Nickel Alloys.
