Industry-to-Material Mapping

Different industries ask for different grades, standards and product forms. The mapping below helps buyers prepare clearer enquiries.

Automotive / EV / auto components

Aluminium 6061/6082/7075, SS 304/316, mild steel, carbon steel, copper and phosphor bronze for components, fixtures and electrical parts.

Chemical / petrochemical

SS 316L, 904L, Duplex 2205, Super Duplex 2507, Hastelloy, Inconel, Titanium Grade 2 and SMO 254 for corrosion-focused RFQs.

Pharma / food processing

SS 304/316L, Titanium Grade 2 and selected nickel alloys for hygienic fabrication, finish, documentation and equipment enquiries.

Marine / offshore / desalination

Duplex, Super Duplex, SMO 254, Titanium, Monel and SS 316L for seawater, chloride and coastal project requirements.

Power / heat / heavy engineering

SS 310/321/347, Inconel, A516 Gr 70, carbon steel and mild steel for heat, pressure and heavy fabrication enquiries.

Construction / infrastructure / fabrication

Mild steel, carbon steel, stainless steel and aluminium for plates, sections, bars, pipes, fasteners and project BOQs.

Electronics / electrical / precision

Copper, brass, phosphor bronze, aluminium and stainless steel for conductivity, machinability and precision part RFQs.

Aerospace / defence / high-performance

Titanium, Inconel, Aluminium 7075, SS 17-4 PH and Nitronic 60 for specification-led enquiries.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Rubinox supply metals across India?

Yes. Rubinox Metal and Alloys is based in Mumbai and can review PAN India RFQs for industrial metals, alloy grades, product forms, documentation requirements and project supply coordination.

Which cities does Rubinox support for metal RFQs?

Rubinox can review enquiries from buyers across major Indian industrial cities and clusters, including Mumbai, Pune, Ahmedabad, Vadodara, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Delhi NCR, Kolkata and other regions listed on this page.

What details should buyers share for a city-based metal quotation?

Share material family, grade, standard, product form, size, quantity, finish, testing or MTC requirement, delivery city and timeline for quotation review.

Which materials are commonly requested by industrial buyers in India?

Common RFQs include stainless steel, duplex, super duplex, nickel alloys, titanium, SMO 254, aluminium, copper, brass, carbon steel, mild steel, phosphor bronze and special engineering alloys.

Can Rubinox support project supply coordination for multiple Indian cities?

Rubinox can review project supply coordination enquiries where buyers share BOQ, MTO, drawings, grade requirements, quantities, documentation needs and delivery locations.

Does Rubinox have branches in every city?

No. Rubinox is based in Mumbai and supports PAN India enquiries. This page lists buyer locations and industrial clusters for RFQ support, not local branch or warehouse locations.

Can buyers request MTC or testing documents?

Buyers can mention MTC, PMI, inspection or testing requirements during RFQ. Documentation assistance is reviewed where applicable and as per buyer or project requirement.

How should buyers choose the right material grade?

Buyers should share application, environment, standard, temperature, corrosion exposure, fabrication method and product form so Rubinox can review the enquiry and suggest suitable material page references.
