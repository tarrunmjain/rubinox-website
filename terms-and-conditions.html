Website Use and Business Positioning

Rubinox Metal and Alloys is positioned as a Stockist | Supplier | Importer | Exporter | Project Supplier for industrial metals and alloy products. Rubinox is not a manufacturer.

Website content is provided for general buyer information, material navigation and RFQ preparation. Users should not treat website content as a substitute for project engineering review or final specification approval.

RFQ and Quotation Process

The website does not publish pricing. Quotations depend on enquiry details such as material, grade, standard, form, size, quantity, finish, documentation, delivery location and sourcing feasibility. Buyers should share complete details for RFQ review.

Availability and Delivery

Availability, quotation response and supply coordination are subject to the enquiry details, sourcing feasibility and project requirements. The website does not provide a delivery guarantee.

Technical Content

Technical content, comparison guidance and standards references are informational. The buyer, consultant, fabricator or project engineer is responsible for confirming grade suitability, design compatibility and standards compliance.

Documentation Assistance

MTC, PMI, inspection and project documentation requests can be discussed where applicable and as per buyer or project requirements. Rubinox does not invent certifications, approvals or vendor status claims.

External Links and Marketplaces

The website may link to third-party websites, maps, social platforms or business directories. These external platforms are controlled by their respective operators and may have separate terms and privacy practices.

Terms & Conditions FAQs

Does the website include prices?

No. The website does not publish pricing. Buyers should send RFQ details for quotation review.

Is Rubinox a manufacturer?

No. Rubinox is a stockist, supplier, importer, exporter and project supplier, not a manufacturer.

Are technical pages final engineering advice?

No. Technical pages are informational and buyers should confirm grade suitability through their project engineer or consultant.

Can buyers request MTC or inspection support?

Buyers can mention MTC, PMI or inspection requirements in the RFQ for review where applicable.

How should buyers send an RFQ to Rubinox?

Buyers should share material, grade or specification, product form, size, quantity, delivery location and documentation requirements so the enquiry can be reviewed clearly.

Can Rubinox support PAN India and export enquiries?

Rubinox can review PAN India and export enquiries with procurement and documentation details shared by the buyer, subject to material, grade, form and sourcing feasibility.
